Another reminder!

theres a pretty bad bug for me at least that im trying to fix very soon. if the homepage configuration (friends tab, show visits) etc doesnt work, logout, log back in, and hard refresh (ctrl+shift+r)

Change configuration

you can change the configuration of everything from the browser popup, by pinning the extension in your toolbar and then clicking on it. if it doesnt open (known bug), keep clicking it until it works.
